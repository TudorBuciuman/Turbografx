<p align="center">
  <strong>$\LARGE \color{#FFE412}{\textsf{BITROOT}}$</strong>
</p>
<p align="center">
  <em>A Top-Down Action RPG, with a CRT effect, based of the Legend of Zelda.</em><br/>
  <em>An origin buried in darkness, pulling the surface down to feed its growth.</em><br/>
  <em>It is the anchor we cannot see, the first cause of every branching path.</em><br/>
  <em>The foundation is a tangled net in the deep, drinking the secrets of the soil.</em><br/>
  <em>We chose the branches, but the blade holds the ultimate truth.</em><br/>
  <em>Every path we take is watered by the same forgotten <b>roots</b>. </em><br/>
</p>
